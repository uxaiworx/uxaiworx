ABOUT

Every product is the result of thousands of decisions.

Some are strategic. Others are technical. Many are invisible to the people using the final product. My work is making those decisions intentional.

For more than 15 years I have helped organizations connect business strategy, user needs, and technology across agency leadership, product strategy, marketing, and emerging technology. Formal UX training gave structure to a process I had already been refining for years.

Today I combine research, systems thinking, AI enabled systems, and human judgment to reduce uncertainty, shorten the distance between ideas and execution, and deliver products that solve meaningful business problems.

AI did not change how I think. It changed how quickly I can move from insight to execution. Human judgment remains at the center. AI simply expands what is possible. The tools continue to evolve. Sound judgment remains the foundation of every successful product I help deliver.

HOW I THINK

Designing Better Decisions

Better product decisions come from viewing the same challenge through multiple lenses. My process connects business goals, user needs, product constraints, and AI supported exploration so the final experience is useful, practical, and built to last.

Business Lens

Start with the outcome.

I start by understanding the business goal, the audience, and the measurable outcome the experience needs to support. Design without business context is decoration.

User Lens

Remove the friction.

I look for confusion, hesitation, unnecessary steps, and moments where users lose confidence or momentum. Friction is where products fail and users walk away.

Product Lens

Simplify the path.

I translate insights into clear user flows, interaction patterns, and product decisions that reduce complexity without removing capability.

AI Lens

Amplify human judgment.

I use AI to support research, ideation, prototyping, documentation, and execution while keeping human judgment at the center of every decision and every deliverable.

GOVERNANCE

AI multiplies output. It doesn't multiply judgment.
