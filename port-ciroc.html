Ciroc Music Site

Client
Ciroc
Project
Music Experience Website
Objective
Branded immersive music experience for a premium spirits brand
Software / Tools
HTML, CSS, Video.js

Designed an immersive, video-first music experience website for Ciroc, the premium spirits brand. The project centered on creating a luxury digital space where music, nightlife, and brand identity converged seamlessly. Video.js powered a custom player that reflected the brand's aesthetic rather than default browser controls, while the overall visual design used cinematic pacing and full-bleed media to deliver the aspirational tone the brand demands.
