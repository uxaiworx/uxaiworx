Synegen

Client
Synegen
Project
Website Refresh
Objective
Full website redesign and ongoing brand and marketing strategy
Software / Tools
HTML, CSS, Angular / SPAs

Synegen had the expertise but the website wasn't communicating it. Through stakeholder interviews I identified the two core differentiators worth leading with and redesigned the site from the ground up, visual system, content hierarchy, page architecture, and component library. Worked alongside the dev team through build and launch, and directed video production to carry the brand tone throughout.

The project grew into an 18 month engagement. Synegen retained me for ongoing design and strategy work spanning social media campaigns, SEO, and trade show branding and marketing. Deploying the full range of my UX and technology skills for one client over that run was a genuinely rewarding experience.
